The Harsh Reality: 27 August se baat shuru toh hui, par dosti se bhi kam… bas formal 'hi/hello'. Aur kal raat jab usne baat-baat me 22 May ka zikr chhed diya, toh saara purana dard fir se taaza ho gaya. Ye wo baatein hain jo zubaan se bolne ki himmat nahi hui… par dil me har pal dhadakti hain.

Kal Raat Ki Yaad • 1 September

1. Jab kal raat tumne 22 May yaad dilaya…

Tap to Open ▼
Kal raat thodi bohot normal baat chal rahi thi… aur achanak jab tumne 22 May ki baat chhed di, mera dil jaise ek pal ke liye ruk gaya. Tumne wo sab kuch yaad dila diya jo us din hua tha — wo ladai, wo gussa, wo shabdon ka chubhna jisne hume itne mahino ke liye ajnabi bana diya. Tumhe shayad lagta hoga ki main wo sab bhool gaya hoon ya mujhe us din ka koi pachtawa nahi hai. Par sach toh ye hai ki pichle 104 dino me ek bhi aisi raat nahi guzri jab maine 22 May ke us ek-ek lamhe ko yaad karke khud ko blame na kiya ho. Kal raat jab tumne wo baat boli na, toh mere paas bolne ke liye koi shabd nahi the. Main screen dekhta raha aur aakhon se aansu behte rahe. Kaash us raat main chup reh jata… kaash hum us din sambhal gaye hote.
Written with a heavy heart — Harsh 🥀
🥀
Distance & Formality • 27 August Se Ab Tak

2. Dosti se bhi kam… bas formal 'hi/hello'

Tap to Open ▼
Maine ye website dikhane ke bahane tumhe message kiya tha 27 August ko. Dil me bas ek choti si umeed thi ki shayad purani Aradhya ki thodi si jhalak dekhne ko mil jaye… Par jab screen par tumhara wo dry aur formal reply dekha — "hii", "haan theek", "achha" — toh samajh aa gaya ki hamare beech ke faasle kitne gehre ho chuke hain. Aaj hum dost bhi nahi hain, bas do ajnabi hain jo bas formality ke liye bolte hain. Ek waqt tha jab hum bina ruke ghanto baatein karte the, ek dusre se bina baat kiye din nahi nikalta tha. Aaj tumhara itna reserved aur cold behave dekh kar andar se cheekh nikal aati hai. Par sach bataun? Tumhare is sookhe se 'hi' ke notification ke liye bhi mera pagal dil subah se raat tak screen check karta rehta hai.
Missing the warmth we had — Tumhara Harsh 🥺

Mera Darr • Unspoken Boundaries

3. Main chup kyu rehta hoon? Mera sabse bada darr

Tap to Open ▼
Main chat me bohot formal rehta hoon na? Jaise bas kaam ki baat kar raha hoon. Tumhe lagta hoga ki main ab badal gaya hoon ya mera dil ab utna nahi feel karta. Par sach ye hai ki main andar se thar-thar kaanpta hoon jab bhi message type karta hoon. Mera dil chahta hai ki tumse puchun — "Khana khaya na time pe?", "Kya soch rahi ho?", "Tumhe meri yaad aati hai kabhi?". Par main apne hothon ko daba leta hoon aur text mita deta hoon. Kyunki mujhe sabse bada darr ye lagta hai ki agar maine thoda bhi haq jataya ya personal baat ki, toh tum gussa ho jaogi aur ye thodi si baat bhi hamesha ke liye chheen logi. Tumse baat na hona meri maut jaisa hai, isliye is formal hi/hello ko bhi khuda ka tohfa samajh ke chup baith jata hoon.
Silent fear of losing you completely — Harsh 💔

Truth & Forgiveness • 22 May

4. 22 May ka sach aur meri dil se maafi

Tap to Open ▼
22 May ko jo hua, main usse bilkul inkaar nahi kar raha. Us raat gusse me aakar jo hum dono ke beech hadd paar hui, aur khaaskar mere munh se jo gaali-galoch aur chubhte huye gande lafz nikle the… uske liye mere paas sharmindgi aur pashchatap ke siva kuch nahi hai. Kasam se upar wale ki, main shayad zindagibhar khud ko maaf nahi kar paunga ki maine us ladki ke samne aisi zubaan use ki jise main mandir ki tarah aadar aur prem deta tha. Gusse me insaan andha ho jaata hai, par wo koi bahaana nahi ho sakta. Jo lafz ek baar munh se nikal jaate hain, wo dil par aisa zakham dete hain jo saalon tak nahi bharta. Tumne jo bola tha na — "Jo ek baar hurt kr deta hai uss baat nhi kr pati 🥹" — wo baat mere seene me teer ki tarah chubhti hai, kyunki tumhara gussa aur tumhari doori 100% sahi hai. Maine tumhe hurt kiya, maine wo samman ki lakeer todi jo prem me kabhi nahi tootni chahiye thi. Agar tum mujhe kabhi maaf na bhi karo, tab bhi main tumhari boundary ki respect karunga. Par itna zaroor jaan lena Aru… us 22 May ki kaali raat ke baad se maine apne gusse aur apni zubaan par kaabu karna seekh liya hai. Main haath jod kar, sar jhuka kar tumse us har ek gande lafz aur har ek aansu ke liye dil ki aakhri gehraai se maafi maangta hoon.
A genuine and unconditional apology — Dil Se Maafi 🙏

Forever Devotion • Unconditional

5. Kyu main kabhi move on nahi kar sakta?

Tap to Open ▼
Log aake lecture dete hain ki 'Move on kar le brother, itne mahine ho gaye, duniya aage badh gayi hai'. Main unhe kya bataun? Unke liye shayad pyaar ek choice hoti hogi, par mere liye tum meri rooh ka hissa ho. Jisse rooh se judao ho jaye, usko dimaag se switch off kaise karun? Chahe tum mujhse kitna bhi gussa raho, chahe tum mujhse ajnabiyo ki tarah hi/hello bolo, chahe tum mujhe 22 May ka zikr karke door karo… Harsh ka dil sirf Aradhya ke naam pe hi dhadkega. Pyaar me koi shart nahi hoti. Tum mujhe wapis chaho ya na chaho, main marte dam tak tumhari khushiyon ki dua mangta rahunga. Main kal bhi tera tha, aaj bhi tera hoon, aur aakhri saans tak sirf tera rahunga.
Yours forever and beyond — Always Yours, Harsh ❤️
